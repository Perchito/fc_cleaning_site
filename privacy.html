2. What data we collect

When you use our contact form or call us, we may collect:

  • Your name and business name

We use your data to:

4. Legal basis

We process your data under the lawful basis of legitimate interests (responding to business enquiries) and contract (delivering agreed services), in line with UK GDPR.

5. Data retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 2 years. Client records related to active contracts are kept for 6 years for legal and accounting purposes, then securely deleted.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

To exercise any right, email us at fernando.c@fccleaningcompany.com.

7. Cookies

This website uses only a strictly necessary session cookie to manage your cookie consent preference. We do not use any analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our commercial cleaning services, scheduling, pricing and how we work.

Getting started

Before you book

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Manchester and cover Greater Manchester and the wider North West, including Salford, Stockport, Bolton, Oldham, Rochdale, Trafford and surrounding areas. If you’re not sure, just ask — we’ll let you know.

What types of businesses do you clean?

We clean offices, restaurants, pubs, bars, cafés, commercial kitchens, takeaways, retail units, and other commercial premises. We work with businesses of all sizes, from small independent venues to larger commercial sites.

Do you offer a free quote?

Yes. All quotes are free and come with no obligation. We’ll ask a few questions about your venue, size, schedule and priorities, and come back with a clear, tailored proposal.

How quickly can you start?

It depends on your requirements and current availability, but we aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours and can often start within a week of agreeing a plan. Get in touch and we’ll give you a realistic timeline.

Scheduling & logistics

How it works

Can you clean outside of business hours?

Yes. We regularly clean early mornings, late evenings and weekends to work around your opening hours and minimise disruption to your team and customers.

How often do you clean?

This depends on your needs. We offer daily, weekly, fortnightly and ad-hoc cleaning schedules. We’ll work with you to find the right frequency for your venue.

Who will I deal with?

You deal directly with the owner. There are no call centres, no account managers, and no handoffs. You get one clear point of contact from your first enquiry through to ongoing service.

What if I need to change my schedule?

Just contact us directly. We’re flexible and will do our best to accommodate changes with as much notice as possible.

Insurance & standards

Your peace of mind

Are you insured?

Yes. FC Cleaning Company is fully insured with public liability insurance. We’re happy to provide details on request.

Do you supply your own cleaning equipment and products?

Yes. We bring our own professional-grade equipment and cleaning products. If your venue has specific product requirements, just let us know and we’ll accommodate where possible.

How do you maintain cleaning standards?

We use structured cleaning routines and direct owner oversight on all accounts. You deal with the owner, not a subcontractor, so standards are maintained consistently.

Pricing

Cost & value

How much does commercial cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your venue, frequency of cleaning, type of service and any specific requirements. We provide a tailored quote after understanding your needs — there’s no standard price list because every venue is different.

Is there a minimum contract length?

We prefer to work on an ongoing basis as it lets us build a proper routine for your venue, but we can discuss one-off or short-term arrangements too. Ask us and we’ll find something that works.
